# Terms of Service Draft

This is an operator draft for review by qualified counsel before paid launch.

## Service Scope

Reactive Research Tools evaluates the structure of arguments, polarity shifts,
scope changes, contradictions, and related research-provenance signals. It is
not a truth oracle and does not provide legal, medical, financial, or scientific
certification advice.

## Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- final decisions based on reports;
- lawful rights to any text submitted to the service;
- not submitting regulated, confidential, or third-party data unless their
  plan and written agreement allow it;
- keeping API keys confidential;
- reviewing outputs before external publication or commercial use.

## Account and API Key Use

API keys are tenant-specific. Customers may not share keys across companies,
projects, or unrelated tenants. The operator may suspend or rotate keys for
abuse, security risk, non-payment, or unlawful use.

## Payment

Paid access begins when payment is accepted or an invoice agreement is active.
Non-payment may result in key suspension. Taxes, refunds, trials, and service
credits must be specified in the final signed/commercial terms.

## Data and Retention

Customer content may include claims, arguments, contexts, probe answers,
advisory seeds, and generated reports. The production privacy notice must state
where this content is stored, the retention period, deletion process, and any
subprocessors.

## Separation

IFRS institutional data and sealed-company material are outside this service
unless an authorized tenant agreement explicitly covers that data.

## No Warranty Draft Language

The service is provided for research operations and argument-structure
analysis. Final warranty, limitation-of-liability, SLA, dispute, governing-law,
and indemnity language must be drafted or approved by counsel.
