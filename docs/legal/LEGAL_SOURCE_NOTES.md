# Legal Source Notes

These are starting points for operator review, not legal advice. Always verify
the current rule for the actual jurisdiction before launch.

## U.S. Business Setup References

- SBA, "Register your business": how and where to register depends on
  structure and business location. It also covers DBA and registered-agent
  basics.
  https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/register-your-business

- IRS, "Starting a business": federal tax topics for new businesses, including
  EIN, structure, business taxes, and recordkeeping links.
  https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/starting-a-business

- IRS Publication 583, "Starting a Business and Keeping Records": practical
  recordkeeping and tax setup reference.
  https://www.irs.gov/publications/p583

- FinCEN, "Beneficial Ownership Information Reporting": current BOI reporting
  source. As of the March 2025 interim final rule described by FinCEN, U.S.
  companies and U.S. persons are treated differently from foreign entities
  registered to do business in the United States; verify before filing or
  deciding not to file.
  https://www.fincen.gov/beneficial-ownership-information-reporting

## Operator Rule

Do not accept paid customers until entity, tax, payment, privacy, retention, and
customer-support obligations have been checked against the current jurisdiction.
